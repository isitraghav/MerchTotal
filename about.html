About Merchtotal

We make custom event merchandise simple, fast, and affordable for organizations of all sizes.

Our Mission

At Merchtotal, we believe that every event deserves high-quality, custom merchandise that brings people together and creates lasting memories. Whether you're organizing a college festival, corporate event, startup launch, or community gathering, we're here to make your merchandise needs effortless.

Founded in 2023, we've helped over 1,200 organizations create custom apparel and accessories that perfectly represent their brand and message. Our streamlined process eliminates the complexity traditionally associated with custom printing, allowing you to focus on what matters most—your event.

How We're Different

Instant Mockups

See your design come to life in real-time. Upload your logo and instantly preview it across different products, fabrics, and colors before placing your order.

Event-Focused

We understand event timelines. Our production and delivery schedules are built around your event date, ensuring your merchandise arrives exactly when you need it.

Flexible Quantities

Whether you need 1 piece or 1,000+, we maintain the same quality and attention to detail. No minimum orders, no compromise on quality.

Quality Materials

We partner with trusted suppliers to offer a curated selection of high-quality fabrics and products, from classic cotton tees to premium fleece hoodies.

Our Team

Our team combines expertise in custom printing, event management, and technology to deliver an exceptional experience. We're a small, dedicated group that personally oversees every order from design to delivery.
